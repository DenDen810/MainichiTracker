Homework generator

Open a student, tap Make this week's homework, and Claude writes a sheet from their plan, goals and notes. It needs your own Anthropic API key, from console.anthropic.com. Usage is billed to you, a few cents a sheet.

claude-sonnet-5 is the default and costs about 5c a sheet. Swap in claude-haiku-4-5 for roughly half that if the writing still reads well to you.

The key is stored on this phone only, in the same place as everything else. Anyone who unlocks the phone can read it, so use a key you can revoke.

Add it to your home screen

You are running this as a Safari tab. That is why the address bar keeps appearing, why the page reloads when you pull down, and why Google Calendar keeps disconnecting: Safari throws background tabs away and every reload loses the connection.

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from there instead and it runs full screen, keeps its connections, and works with no signal.

What the app is carrying

Every worksheet you have ever made is stored in full and read back on every launch. Old ones are the usual reason the app gets heavy enough that your phone closes it in the background.

Tidy up

For anything brought in before: goes through every prepay bundle oldest first and moves the lessons it paid for onto it, rewriting their charge to the bundle rate. Only touches lessons on or after the bundle\u2019s own date that are not already on one.

Finds lessons with the same student, date and time, keeps one, and deletes the calendar events for the rest.
